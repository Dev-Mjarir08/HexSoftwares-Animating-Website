Our Story

Rooted In Craft

From a single atelier to an international house — every chapter grew from the same root: uncompromising craftsmanship.

1998

The First Stitch

APPLE begins in a small atelier with one tailor, one vision — clothing stripped of excess.

2006

The Atelier Grows

A workshop of master tailors joins, refining construction techniques passed down for generations.

2014

First Runway

APPLE steps onto the international stage, presenting its first runway collection to critical acclaim.

2023

Flagship Opens

Our first flagship house opens its doors — a physical expression of the APPLE philosophy.
